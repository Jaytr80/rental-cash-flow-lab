A worksheet cannot inspect a roof, read a lease for you, or confirm loan terms.

Match the property’s claims against leases, income and expense records, tax information, insurance indications, title documents, and professional inspections. Separate routine repairs from major replacement work. Get written scopes and bids where appropriate.

For a rehab or BRRRR plan, think about the exit first. A refinance can produce less cash than expected, arrive later, or fail to close. Ask the lender about seasoning, valuation, income coverage, condition, and documentation requirements. Prepare a backup plan that does not depend on a particular appraisal.

Today’s task: make a short list of unresolved items, the person responsible for each, and the date you need an answer. Let missing evidence remain visible.
