Before counting properties, write down the monthly spending you want your plan to support. Include a buffer and costs you might otherwise overlook. Subtract reliable non-rental income available for that goal. The remaining gap is a planning input, not a deadline.

A rough property count can be explored by dividing the annual gap by a conservative positive annual cash-flow estimate per property. This shortcut cannot account for uneven repairs, different properties, taxes, or correlated vacancies. It is a prompt to investigate, not a forecast.

Also ask how much cash you can keep available and how much time you can give to ownership.
