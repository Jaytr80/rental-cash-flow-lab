preview: "A promising property still belongs to a local market."

Define the area and property type you are researching. Gather dated rent comparables, learn who pays utilities, and ask local managers about turnover and leasing friction.

Check local rental licensing, inspections, zoning, and occupancy requirements through official sources and appropriate professionals. Investigate insurance availability and property-specific hazards. Look at competing supply and employment concentration without assuming that past conditions will continue.

Keep a short research log: question, source, date, finding, and what remains unknown. A market screen should be useful even when it tells you to pause.

Today’s task: list three market questions that could change your expense or vacancy assumptions, then identify where you will verify them.
