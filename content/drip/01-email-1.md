A listing gives you a starting point. Your analysis needs more.

Compare rent with relevant properties, and note concessions and differences in condition. Include vacancy and collection loss even when a unit is currently occupied. Ask for current insurance indications and verify property taxes rather than copying last year’s bill without checking reassessment.

Keep management and routine repairs visible. Put capital replacements in a separate reserve. Do not count taxes and insurance again inside debt service if you already included them as operating expenses.

For each major assumption, write the source, date, and what would make it wrong. Then test lower rent and higher expenses together. A deal that needs everything to go right deserves more questions.
